Compliance — GDPR Policy (Internal)
- All personal data collected from EU residents must have a documented lawful basis.
- Data retention periods: customer records 7 years, employee records 10 years.
- Data subject access requests must be fulfilled within 30 days.
- Privacy impact assessments are mandatory for any new product that processes personal data.
- Third-party processors must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before receiving personal data.
- The Data Protection Officer (DPO) must be consulted on all high-risk processing activities.
