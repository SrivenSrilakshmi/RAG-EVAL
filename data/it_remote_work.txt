IT Policy — Remote Work Infrastructure (Internal)
- All remote employees must connect via corporate VPN for accessing internal systems.
- Company-issued laptops are encrypted with BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault (macOS).
- Personal devices may not access production systems under any circumstances.
- VPN split-tunneling is disabled to ensure all traffic routes through corporate security filters.
- Remote desktop sessions are terminated automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
- IT support for remote workers is available 24/7 via the helpdesk portal.
