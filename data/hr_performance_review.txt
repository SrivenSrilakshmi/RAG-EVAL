HR Policy — Performance Reviews (Internal)
- Annual performance reviews are conducted in January each year.
- Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds, Meets, Developing, Unsatisfactory.
- Ratings of Unsatisfactory trigger a mandatory Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) within 30 days.
- Promotion decisions are made during the April calibration cycle.
- Manager feedback must be submitted two weeks before the employee's review meeting.
- All ratings are confidential and accessible only to the employee, their manager, and HR.
