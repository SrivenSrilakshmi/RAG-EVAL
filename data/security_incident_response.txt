Security Policy — Incident Response (Internal)
- Security incidents must be reported to the CISO within 1 hour of detection.
- A Severity 1 security incident triggers mandatory isolation of affected systems within 15 minutes.
- The incident response team lead must be notified via the dedicated PagerDuty channel.
- All evidence must be preserved; no affected systems may be rebooted without IR team approval.
- Post-incident reports are due within 5 business days of containment.
- GDPR data breach notifications to regulators are required within 72 hours if personal data is involved.
