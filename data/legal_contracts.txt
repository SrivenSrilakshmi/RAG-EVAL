Legal Policy — Contracts & NDAs (Internal)
- All vendor contracts above 50,000 USD require Legal review before signing.
- Non-disclosure agreements must be signed before sharing product roadmap details.
- Contract renewals must be flagged to Legal 90 days before expiry.
- Governing law for all agreements is the State of Delaware unless negotiated otherwise.
- Arbitration is the mandatory dispute resolution mechanism for B2B contracts.
