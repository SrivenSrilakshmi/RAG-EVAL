Security Policy — Access Control (Internal)
- Privileged access to production systems requires MFA using a hardware token.
- Access reviews are conducted quarterly; unused accounts are revoked automatically.
- Service accounts must follow the principle of least privilege.
- SSH access to production servers is restricted to the VPN and logged.
- The emergency break-glass account credentials are stored in the offline vault under dual control.
- Privileged account passwords are rotated every 90 days.
