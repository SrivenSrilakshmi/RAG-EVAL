Legal Policy — Intellectual Property (Internal)
- All code written by employees belongs to the company under work-for-hire rules.
- Open-source components must be approved by Legal if they use GPL v3 or AGPL licenses.
- Patent filings must be submitted within 12 months of invention disclosure.
- Trademarks must be registered in all markets where revenue exceeds 1 million USD.
- Employees may not file personal patents on work-related inventions without prior written approval.
