Finance Policy — Expense Reimbursement (Internal)
- Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense.
- Meals with clients are reimbursable up to 75 USD per person with itemized receipts.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is a pre-approved client entertainment event.
- Personal vehicle mileage reimbursement rate is 0.67 USD per mile (IRS standard 2025).
- All expenses above 500 USD require manager pre-approval before being incurred.
- Corporate card misuse results in immediate suspension and possible termination.
