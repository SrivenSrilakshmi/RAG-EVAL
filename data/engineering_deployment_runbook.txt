Engineering — Deployment Runbook (Internal)
- Production deployments are restricted to Tuesday–Thursday between 10:00–16:00 UTC.
- All deployments require a rollback plan documented in the Jira ticket.
- Blue-green deployments are the standard pattern for services with SLA commitments.
- Post-deployment smoke tests must complete successfully before traffic is shifted.
- Database migrations must be backwards-compatible for at least one release cycle.
- On-call engineer must be notified 30 minutes before any deployment begins.
