HR Policy — Benefits (Internal)
- Medical insurance coverage begins on the first day of employment.
- The company contributes 80% of the premium for employee-only medical plans.
- 401(k) matching: company matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregiver, 6 weeks for secondary caregiver.
- Tuition reimbursement is capped at 5,250 USD per calendar year.
- Employees are eligible for the Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) after 90 days.
